Types of Favorite Words

The results from the initial data of participants shows that 31.3% chose swear words as their favorite expression, 64.1% chose other regular expressions, and 4.7% named word particles that adds expressiveness of a sentence as their favorite Cantonese expression.

Significance of Cantonese by Keywords

We asked our participant what the significance of Cantonese is from a linguistic or personal standpoint. We analyzed the results qualitatively and created related keywords from studying their responses. Majority expressed more than one significance.

"Sound Bites" is a community based research project dedicated to collecting, recording, and preserving distinctive Cantonese soundscape of Hong Kong. Our archive features favorite Cantonese expressions of Hongkongers from various background and ages. Through interactive, illuminated neon sign visuals, we hope to convey these warm, humorous, and historically significant everyday expressions to everyone. Click on any sign to hear the authentic street pronunciation and explanation.
