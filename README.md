# project web

一個小型的音聲研究項目網站。

目前囊括的內容包括：
* **香港廣東話本土研究**
* **「Sound Bites」**：用短短一個字詞或句子的語音，從一個很日常輕鬆並具趣味性的角度，分享人們對這門語言的喜愛。聆聽各個分享者如何演繹自己最心愛的詞彙，以及體驗這門獨特語言所帶來的人情味。

---

A small-scale research project website focusing on audio and sound.

Currently featuring:
* **Local studies on Hong Kong Cantonese**
* **"Sound Bites"**: Short voice recordings of words or sentences that share people's love for this language from a casual, relaxed, and engaging perspective. Listen to different contributors interpret their favorite expressions and experience the human warmth brought by this unique language.
